Our Endocrinology Service

Expert Hormonal & Metabolic Care

Our endocrinology service covers three deeply related specialties under a single consultant: general endocrinology, diabetes management, and obesity medicine. This integrated approach allows comprehensive metabolic assessment and coordinated treatment — avoiding fragmented care across multiple providers.

Every patient is seen personally by Dr Syed Kashif Hussain Kazmi — a Fellow of the Royal College of Physicians with training at Addenbrooke's Hospital, Cambridge, and on the Specialist Register in both Ireland and the UK.

"Endocrinology, diabetes and obesity are three faces of the same metabolic story — we treat all three together."

Most endocrine conditions exist on a metabolic continuum. Insulin resistance drives not only type 2 diabetes, but obesity, PCOS, dyslipidaemia, and cardiovascular risk. Thyroid disease affects weight, cardiac rhythm, and bone density. Treating each in isolation misses the wider picture.

Our service provides a genuinely integrated metabolic assessment — considering the hormonal, glycaemic, and weight-related dimensions of every patient's health — and coordinates with Dr Kalyar's cardiology service when cardiometabolic risk requires a combined approach.

Thyroid Disorders

Comprehensive thyroid assessment from initial diagnosis through to long-term management, including specialist thyroid imaging interpretation.

  • Hypothyroidism — diagnosis, levothyroxine optimisation

Pituitary Disorders

Assessment of pituitary function, hormone deficiencies, and pituitary lesions, with coordination of specialist neurological and surgical input where required.

  • Prolactinaemia and prolactinoma management
  • Cushing's disease screening and monitoring
  • Acromegaly assessment and follow-up
  • Diabetes insipidus evaluation
  • Post-pituitary surgery endocrine management

Evaluation and management of adrenal gland pathology, including incidentally discovered adrenal masses and functional adrenal syndromes.

  • Cushing's syndrome — diagnosis and management
  • Primary hyperaldosteronism (Conn's syndrome)
  • Phaeochromocytoma screening
  • Secondary adrenal insufficiency — steroid withdrawal

Calcium & Bone Disorders

Specialist management of calcium and bone metabolism disorders, vitamin D deficiency, and related conditions affecting skeletal health.

  • Vitamin D deficiency — assessment and replacement
  • Metabolic bone disease including osteoporosis workup
  • Paget's disease and bone turnover assessment

Expert evaluation and management of testosterone deficiency and male reproductive endocrine disorders.

PMOS / PCOS & Female Hormones

Assessment of polycystic ovary syndrome and related metabolic complications, alongside other female endocrine presentations.

Type 1 Diabetes

Expert management of type 1 diabetes including advanced insulin technologies, CGM optimisation, and management of hypoglycaemia unawareness.

GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Therapy

Dr Kazmi has prescribed and personally monitored GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy in over 3,500 patients in the past year alone — one of the most extensive clinical GLP-1RA portfolios in Ireland. This depth of experience spans initiation, dose titration, tolerability management, side-effect monitoring, and long-term structured follow-up across Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro and Saxenda. All prescribing is evidence-based and consultant-supervised at every visit.

Medication safety: Prescription medications including GLP-1 receptor agonists, tirzepatide, insulin, SGLT2 inhibitors, testosterone therapy and thyroid treatments require full medical assessment. Treatment is subject to eligibility, contraindication review, medication availability and shared decision-making, and may not be suitable for every patient.

Investigations Arranged

Diagnostics & Testing

A comprehensive range of endocrine and metabolic investigations, arranged promptly and reviewed personally at each follow-up consultation.

Results are reviewed in full context — not just flagged as 'abnormal' — with a clear management plan discussed with the patient and communicated to their GP.

  • 01
    HbA1c & Full Glucose Profiling

    Glycated haemoglobin, fasting glucose, OGTT, and CGM time-in-range analysis for comprehensive diabetes assessment.

  • 02
    Full Thyroid Function Panel

    TSH, free T4, free T3, anti-TPO, anti-thyroglobulin, and thyroid-stimulating immunoglobulins as clinically indicated.

  • 03
    Adrenal Function Testing

    Cortisol, ACTH, 24-hour urinary cortisol, short synacthen test, and aldosterone/renin ratio.

  • 04
    Pituitary Hormone Panel

    IGF-1, prolactin, FSH, LH, testosterone, SHBG, GH stimulation testing as indicated.

  • 05
    Calcium, PTH & Vitamin D

    Adjusted calcium, ionised calcium, PTH, 25-OH vitamin D, phosphate, and 24-hour urinary calcium.

  • 06
    CGM Reports & Download Analysis

    Ambulatory glucose profile (AGP) analysis, time-in-range assessment, and insulin dose optimisation.

  • 07
    Metabolic & Lipid Panel

    Fasting lipids, LDL/HDL, triglycerides, hsCRP, insulin level, full renal and hepatic function.

  • 08
    Imaging Referrals

    Thyroid ultrasound, adrenal CT/MRI, pituitary MRI, DEXA scan — arranged promptly at partnered imaging centres.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a GP referral?
A GP referral letter is recommended and assists with clinical triage and prioritisation. Self-referrals may be accepted at the clinic's discretion. Please contact us to discuss your situation.
Do you manage thyroid disease?
Yes. We manage all common thyroid conditions including hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, Graves' disease, thyroid nodules and thyroiditis. Specialist review of persistent symptoms despite standard treatment is also available.
Do you prescribe GLP-1 medications?
GLP-1 receptor agonist medications may be considered for eligible patients following full medical assessment. Prescribing is subject to clinical eligibility, contraindication review, medication availability and shared decision-making.
Can you review complex diabetes?
Yes. We provide specialist management of type 1, type 2 and complex diabetes including review of CGM data, insulin optimisation, technology-supported diabetes management and cardiovascular risk reduction.
Do you review PMOS / PCOS?
Yes. We offer specialist assessment and management of PMOS / PCOS including metabolic risk assessment, hormonal evaluation and evidence-based management of insulin resistance and related conditions.
Do you manage testosterone deficiency?
Yes. We assess and manage male hypogonadism and testosterone deficiency following appropriate investigation and clinical assessment. Treatment is subject to eligibility and shared decision-making.
Can you review CGM, Libre, Dexcom or insulin pump reports?
Yes. We are experienced in reviewing continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) data including FreeStyle Libre, Dexcom and insulin pump reports. Please bring printed or digital reports to your appointment.
