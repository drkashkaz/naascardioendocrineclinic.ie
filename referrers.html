Referral Information

For GPs &
Referring Clinicians

We welcome referrals from GPs, practice nurses, and specialist colleagues across Kildare and the wider Leinster region. Prompt appointments, comprehensive specialist assessment, and structured clinic letters returned to your practice.

Referral Process

How to Refer

Our referral process is straightforward. Healthmail is the preferred method for sending patient-identifiable clinical information securely.

01
Send Your Referral

Send a referral letter via Healthmail to Syed.Kazmi@healthmail.ie. Include a brief clinical summary, current medications, and relevant investigation results. Telephone referrals accepted on 089 656 7597.

02
Acknowledgement

You will receive acknowledgement within 2 working days. We will contact your patient directly to arrange an appointment at a mutually convenient time. Sundays 9am–5pm, Vista Primary Care Clinic, Naas.

03
Consultant Assessment

Your patient receives a thorough consultant-delivered assessment. Relevant investigations may be arranged on the day. A same-sex chaperone is available on request — please indicate in your referral if required.

04
Clinic Letter to You

A structured clinic letter is sent promptly following consultation, summarising the assessment, investigations, diagnosis, and management plan. Results and investigation reports are copied to the referring clinician.

Referral Content

What to Include

Including the following information helps us prioritise your referral and prepare a focused assessment for your patient.

Referral Checklist

Incomplete referrals may cause delays. Where investigations are not yet available, please state this clearly.

Patient demographicsFull name, date of birth, address, contact number
Your contact detailsGP name, practice address and Healthmail address for reply
Clinical summaryCurrent symptoms, duration, relevant past medical and family history
Current medicationsAll medications with doses, including OTC and supplements
Relevant blood resultsWith dates — HbA1c, thyroid function, lipid profile, renal function, LFTs, FBC as appropriate
Previous investigationsECG, echocardiogram, thyroid ultrasound, DEXA scan, ambulatory BP — as available
Your clinical questionThe specific concern or question you would like the consultant to address
Degree of urgencyRoutine, soon, or urgent — with brief clinical justification if urgent

What Happens Next

After Your Referral

We aim to make the experience smooth for both referring clinicians and patients, with clear communication at every stage.

1
Referral Acknowledged

We aim to acknowledge all referrals within 2 working days of receipt. If additional clinical information is required, we will contact you promptly via Healthmail or telephone.

2
Patient Contacted for Appointment

Your patient will be contacted directly to arrange a convenient appointment. Clinic operates on Sundays 9am–5pm at Suite 5, Vista Primary Care Clinic, Ballymore Road, Naas, Co. Kildare.

3
Comprehensive Consultant Assessment

Your patient receives a thorough consultant-delivered assessment. Relevant investigations may be arranged at the time of consultation. A same-sex chaperone is available on request.

4
Clinic Letter Returned to You

A comprehensive clinic letter is sent to the referring clinician following consultation, summarising the assessment findings, investigation results, diagnosis, and management plan. Copies are provided to any additional named clinicians as appropriate.

⚡  Urgent Referrals: For clinically urgent cases requiring an expedited appointment, please telephone 089 656 7597 or send a priority-flagged Healthmail to Syed.Kazmi@healthmail.ie with "URGENT" in the subject line. For life-threatening emergencies, please direct the patient to the Emergency Department or call 999 / 112.
