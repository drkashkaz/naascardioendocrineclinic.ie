# Naas Cardiology & Endocrinology Clinic Website

A professional static website for **Naas Cardiology & Endocrinology Clinic**, a private consultant-led specialist clinic based in Naas, Co. Kildare, Ireland.

The website presents private specialist services in:

* Cardiology
* Endocrinology
* Diabetes
* Obesity Medicine
* Cardiometabolic risk management

The clinic is based at:

**Suite 5, Vista Primary Care Clinic**
**Ballymore Road, Naas**
**Co. Kildare, W91 E6H2**
**Ireland**

---

## Website Purpose

This website is designed to provide patients, GPs, and referring clinicians with clear information about the clinic, its consultants, available services, referral pathways, appointment enquiries, and patient safety information.

The website also aims to support local SEO for patients searching for private cardiology, endocrinology, diabetes, obesity medicine, thyroid, PCOS/PMOS, chest pain, hypertension, and cardiometabolic care in Naas, Co. Kildare and the wider Leinster region.

## Compliance and Safety Notes

This website is for informational purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

The clinic does not provide emergency medical care. Patients with urgent symptoms, including severe chest pain, sudden breathlessness, collapse, stroke symptoms or any acute medical emergency, should call **999 or 112** or attend the nearest Emergency Department.

For privacy and GDPR reasons, website forms should not collect detailed clinical information unless a secure and appropriate healthcare data-processing workflow is in place.

GP referral letters and clinical correspondence should be sent securely via Healthmail where appropriate.

## Recommended Pre-Launch Checklist

Before promoting the website publicly, check:

* All pages load without 403 or 404 errors
* SSL certificate is valid and auto-renewing
* Canonical URLs match the live domain
* Sitemap uses the correct domain
* `robots.txt` is publicly accessible
* All internal links work
* Mobile menu works on Android and iPhone
* Appointment form works correctly
* Cookie banner functions correctly
* Privacy policy is up to date
* Emergency disclaimer is visible
* Images are compressed and loading correctly
* Doctor photos have appropriate alt text
* Footer information is consistent across pages
* Google Search Console is configured
* Google Business Profile is created and linked
