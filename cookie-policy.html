What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function correctly and improve your experience.

How we use cookies

This website may use essential cookies to ensure basic functionality. We do not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. If we introduce analytics cookies in future, this policy will be updated and your consent will be sought via the cookie consent banner.

Essential cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function. They include cookies that record your cookie consent choice and standard browser session management cookies. These cannot be disabled.

Analytics cookies

If you accept analytics cookies via our cookie consent banner, Google Analytics 4 may be activated. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage (key: cookieConsent) and refreshing the page.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality. For more information about managing cookies, visit www.aboutcookies.org.
