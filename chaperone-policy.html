Your right to a chaperone

Patients are entitled to request a chaperone for any clinical examination or consultation at this clinic. This right applies without exception and does not require any reason to be given.

Who can act as a chaperone

A chaperone may be a clinic staff member arranged by the clinic, or a person of the patient's own choosing such as a family member, friend, or carer. The patient may choose who accompanies them.

How to request a chaperone

Please inform reception when booking your appointment if you wish a chaperone to be arranged by the clinic. You are also welcome to bring a person of your choosing to any appointment.

Confidentiality

Any chaperone present during a clinical consultation is expected to maintain patient confidentiality. Clinic-arranged chaperones are bound by professional confidentiality obligations.

Questions

If you have any questions about our chaperone policy, please contact the clinic prior to your appointment.
